## Project description

Plant Leaf Disease Detection Using Image Processing was my undergraduate project, completed from April 2022 to July 2022 for a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Kallam Haranadhareddy Institute of Technology. The program studies a leaf photograph with image processing. It resizes the image, reduces noise, separates the leaf from the background, and estimates how much of that region differs from a green reference. The result is a visual check of the photograph. It does not name a disease and it does not report an accuracy figure.
